# Why I chose Grimmory over Audiobookshelf — 2026-07-09

## Context
Need a self-hosted service to organize and read books.

## Decision
Grimmory has better library organization than Audiobookshelf for my case.
Magic shelves, automatic metadata, built-in reader for EPUBs/PDFs/comics,
OPDS support. GUI felt better too.

## Why not BookLore (the original)
BookLore disappeared overnight in 2026 after AI-generated code concerns
and conflicts with the dev. Grimmory formed days later as a community
fork. BookLore is technically back now under new maintainers, but
sticking with Grimmory since it already fits what I need.

## Note
Some users report Grimmory as more resource heavy than lighter
alternatives. Something to watch given the laptop's limited RAM.
